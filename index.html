Water shouldn't be a gamble.

Beyond Thirst is a four-person action project mapping where safe water runs out — and building the smallest possible interventions that make the biggest difference for the people waiting in line for it.

The problem

Thirst isn't a shortage. It's a distribution failure.

In much of Bangladesh, water is close by but not safe: rising salinity is pushing into coastal aquifers, and naturally occurring arsenic contaminates millions of tube wells inland. Beyond Thirst starts by measuring exactly where those two crises overlap with the communities least able to absorb them.

Why "Beyond Thirst"

We're not just documenting a crisis.

Most water campaigns stop at raising awareness. We wanted ours to also produce something usable: a public map, a tested low-cost filter design, and a short brief any local council could act on this year.

Four of us split the work across research, field testing, storytelling and outreach — this site is where all four strands meet.

  • → Ground-truthed field data, not secondhand statistics
  • → Low-cost interventions communities can maintain themselves
  • → Open data others can build on after we're done
  • → A clear ask for the people who control the budget
